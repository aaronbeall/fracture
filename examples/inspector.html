The algorithm

  • Randomly spaced radial cracks split the surface into wedges.
  • Each wedge is recursively bisected by straight cuts, angled off the radial direction and placed off-center.
  • Splitting stops below a target area that grows with distance² from the impact, scaled by a random grain each branch inherits.
  • Pieces stay convex and tile the original exactly.

Initial force · resulting 3D speed

0 · weak
  • Strength falls off exponentially with distance from the impact, to about 37% every 180px.
  • Pieces are pushed outward, with a sideways swirl and a push toward the viewer.
  • Hits near an edge add a shared push toward the element's center.
  • Each piece gets ±40% strength and a slightly randomized direction and spin.
  • Speed and spin are capped; colors use px/s of simulation time.
